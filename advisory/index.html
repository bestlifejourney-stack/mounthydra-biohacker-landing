The record

Every lesson here was paid for at the operating level.

I've spent four decades in the chief executive's chair — growing, fixing, and selling companies across software, healthcare technology, financial services technology, and fulfillment. CEO of Why.com and bConnected Software, Chairman & President of Accupost, founder and CEO of Falcon Fulfillment. Of the twelve startup and turnaround companies I've helped lead, five were acquired and two went public on NASDAQ before acquisition — over $2 billion in shareholder value created along the way.

Since 2005 I've been Managing Partner of Arilex Ventures, a private holding company that acquires and operates businesses in health & wellness, healthcare technology, and financial technology.

I still operate. Today I own and run Mount Hydra (regenerative medicine), SCEQL Labs (a bioscience laboratory in Irvine, California), and 3D Exosomes — a portfolio run by a lean human team and a working AI agent workforce that handles finance, operations, and marketing execution. When I advise on AI‑native operations, it's from practice, not theory.

Operating principles

The rules I run my own companies by.

"Never put a human in the path of execution."

Automate relentlessly. People belong on judgment calls, not data entry. My own companies run day-to-day on AI agents so the humans can think.

"Have opinions. Flag risks. Don't be a yes‑machine."

An advisor who agrees with everything is a cost center. You're paying for the disagreements.

"Double-check the math."

Every growth story is won or lost in the unit economics. I read the numbers before I read the narrative.
